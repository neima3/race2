## Phase 3 — Traffic rush (2026-09-14, commit `v8 Phase 3`)

**Shipped**
- `TrafficManager` (src/systems/traffic.ts): 8-12 kinematic traffic cars per track, deterministic via `mulberry32(hashSeed('traffic:' + trackId))` — same layout every race on a track. Each car is `{dist, lane, speed, paint}`; stepping is `dist += v·dt` (wrapped) along a fixed lane, NO CarPhysics. Pace = 40 m/s reference × 55-70% band, low-biased (rng²) so closing speeds clear the 12 m/s near-miss bar. Lanes alternate left/right, edge-biased 1.8-3.2 m (keeps the center racing line open for squeezes). Loop-respawn: any car >80 m behind the player re-spawns +120 m ahead → count constant (asserted in test). Player↔traffic contact band |Δdist| < 3.2 && |Δlat| < 1.7 → strong scrub on the player only (`vel × (1−6·dt)` per step, stronger than the 3.5 mover scrub) + once per 0.8 s cooldown per car: spark burst at contact midpoint, `audio.crash()` thud hook (P7 replaces with a dedicated thud), rumble + shake, traffic car nudged ±2 m laterally for ~1 s then smoothstep-eased back (clamped to road half-width). NO physics damage, NO race end, NO traffic slowdown (kinematic).
- Near miss: per-car pass state machine — on ahead→behind crossing (|rel| < 10 m guards against respawn/teleport false-positives) a ±0.4 s window opens; if min|Δlat| < 2.2 m, closing speed > 12 m/s, and no collision inside the window → NEAR MISS chip (center flash) + HUD counter bump. Max 5 credited per race (counter shows raw count with 5+ display cap; finish panel credits `min(n, 5)`); applied ONLY at the finish panel — `elapsedMs` untouched mid-race.
- TRAFFIC mode chip (menus.ts) joins DRIFT/RIVALS/KNOCKOUT, fully exclusive; career/daily/friend flows force-clear it; `startTrack` guard `careerRace/dailyRace === null` mirrors knockout. Traffic race = 1 lap, time-trial checkpoint/finish rules (92% anti-cheat intact), `writesRecords = false`, ghosts forced 0 (no PB/ghost/drift/lifetime writes anywhere in the finish path). Finish panel: raw time, `NEAR MISSES n (c CREDITED) · BONUS −X.XXs`, `SCORE` (bonus-adjusted), per-track best line or `🏆 NEW TRAFFIC BEST` banner; medal/splits/history/PB rows and WATCH REPLAY/SHARE GHOST buttons suppressed; RETRY / NEXT TRACK (stays in traffic mode) / TRACK SELECT remain.

**Deviations / notes**
1. Collision lateral band is 1.7 m, not the task's 2.2 m — with a 2.2 m contact band a near miss (<2.2 m) is geometrically unreachable because every pass crosses Δdist ≈ 0. The 1.7 m car-width contact band + 2.2 m near-miss band creates the intended clean-squeeze corridor (documented in traffic.ts). Dist band stays 3.2 m.
2. Traffic pace is low-biased inside the 55-70% band (rng², effective ~55-62%): a uniform band makes closing speeds fall under the 12 m/s near-miss threshold vs a ~35-40 m/s player. Roadmap P9 balance pass may revisit.
3. Lanes alternate sides (spec) but are edge-biased 1.8-3.2 m instead of hugging center — center lanes put every car on the autopilot racing line and produce all collisions / zero near misses.
4. `trafficBest` stores the bonus-adjusted score (the roadmap's finish-panel "score"), not the raw time.
5. WATCH REPLAY is hidden in traffic because `writesRecords=false` records no replay samples (same suppression path as rival modes) — consistent, not a regression.
6. All 13 gates re-run green after the final tuning pass; steering-direction convention untouched (no car.ts/curve.ts changes — traffic orients via the podium-proven `(normal×tangent, normal, tangent)` basis).
